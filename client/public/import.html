Manage Donations

Paste a list of pledges from Excel, Google Sheets, or a CSV, check it in the sheet below, then record every ready row in one go. Each row becomes a normal manual gift: it reaches the ballroom screen after the usual delay and can be edited or deleted in Manage Donations. Online gifts are imported automatically; do not paste them here.

1. Bring in the rows

Include the header row and columns are matched by name: Donor name (or First name and Last name), Amount, Anonymous, Pronunciation, Table, Card number, Note. Other columns are ignored.

Without a header row, columns are taken in the order shown in the sheet. You can also paste straight into the sheet, or type rows by hand.

An .xlsx file cannot be opened directly: copy the cells in Excel and paste them here.

2. Check the sheet

No rows yet.

Right-click or long-press a row number to insert or delete rows. Tick Confirm only when the Status column asks for it.

3. Record

Rows marked Ready are recorded when you press Record gifts; you review the list first. Rows that need attention stay in the sheet with the reason in their Status column.
