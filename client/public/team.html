Team and backups

Who can sign in, how they get in, and the snapshots that protect the ledger. Administrators only.

Operator accounts

Create one account per person. Then either tell them the name and PIN, copy a one-time Sign-in link to text them, or send an Email invite with the link and the steps for the night. Links work once and expire after 7 days. Disabling an account ends their session immediately.

Backups and restore

Loading snapshots…

Each snapshot is a complete copy of the database, safe to download while the event runs. Restore replaces every gift, setting, and team note with the snapshot (a pre-restore snapshot is taken first); operator accounts are never touched. The per-gift Delete and Restore on History are usually the better tool; use a full restore only after a serious mistake.
